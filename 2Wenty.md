Our philosophy

A country is not a museum. It wakes before sunrise, commutes, queues for trains. We'd like you to see that.

Most travel is sold by quantity: more cities, more monuments, more photographs in fewer days. You come home remembering where you went and little of how it felt.

2Wenty is built the other way round. A traveller knows they are a guest. They observe before speaking, adapt before expecting, and enter unfamiliar places with curiosity.

### Fewer places, understood better

We'd rather you remember how a place made you feel than how many you ticked off.

### The train is part of the destination

Public transport and long walks show you how a city lives, so we plan around them.

### Room between the highlights

Space to pause, get lost without getting anxious, and go back to a street because it felt welcoming.

### Freedom inside a frame

Enough guidance to feel secure, enough independence to feel alive. Every route is walked before you walk it.

Every traveller agrees

### The Traveller's Pact

A shared understanding, not rules from above. Not everyone is meant to travel with us, and that is fine.

- We keep our voices low on trains and respect silence where it is valued.
- We wait in queues without complaint.
- We don't turn temples, stations or streets into stages for social media.
- We leave no litter for others to collect.
- We remember we are guests, and that how we behave speaks for everyone who comes after us.
